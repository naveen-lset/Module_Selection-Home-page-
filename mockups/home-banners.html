What to look at before deciding

  • How much of the module grid it pushes down. A is 272px plus dots. B is 312px. C is 44px of control plus 256px. D1 and D3 add nothing; D2 adds 72px.
  • Whether Key Insights should ever be hidden. In A they are one dot away; in C, one tap; in B, always visible.
  • The Notes blue. It is the phone's gradient, kept on purpose so a Note is recognisable across devices. It is the loudest thing on this page.
  • The Pending Allocations carousel from the phone is not in any of these; it is a Site attention row and belongs with the Site widgets.
