Quick Actions, six other ideas

The ten before this were all the same idea: a panel that lists sixteen verbs, in ten arrangements. Rejected, correctly. Each of the six here deletes a different premise instead, and the premise it deletes is printed on it in red.

Every one is drawn inside a 1024×768 iPad in landscape, at real size, over the page it opens on. The subjects are the app's own — Big Bull is AAID : 87546/24 and Hoofstock Barn is ENC : HB-04, both from observations.js — and no glyph is invented, which is why 5 draws a waveform and not a microphone.

Which of the six, and what each one costs

IdeaBest whenFalls down whenBuild
1 Scan to actYou are standing in front of the animalNo tag to scan, or a verb with no subjectResolver exists; needs a state → verb table

3 On the cardThe home page is already the map of the workThe card you need is not on this pageCheapest of the six — twelve cards, one popover
4 One verbOne action dominates a role's dayNobody has measured which one that isTrivial, and untrustworthy until measured
5 Say itGloves, a hose, one hand, an enclosureA noisy house, or a name the parser mishearsDictation plus a parser — the most new machinery
6 One composerThe keeper should not have to know the taxonomyThe classifier guesses wrong and files it silentlyNeeds a classifier, and an undo you can trust
  • 1 and 3 are the pair I would build. They answer the two halves of the same question — 3 when the subject is already on screen, 1 when it is in front of you and not on screen — and between them the sixteen-verb panel never has to exist. Neither needs a service that does not exist yet.
  • 2 is the most valuable and the least ready. "What is due" is what a command centre is for, but the app has no due-work service to ask; Pending Approvals is the only queue it can currently fill a row from.
  • 5 is the only one that changes who can use the product. A keeper with wet gloves cannot type, and that is not an edge case in this building — it is the job. Worth a real trial even though it is the most machinery.
  • The four verbs with no subject — + Site, + Section, + User, Master — belong under the avatar in every one of these six. That menu already says it holds everything a person can do to their own page; the estate growing is that kind of act, not a keeper's.
