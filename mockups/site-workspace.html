Notes on this mockup

One thing here departs from a settled decision. §15 asks for soft tonal gradients and controlled saturation; the product's cards are the full-saturation Figma ramps with white text, and re-opening that was explicitly ruled out. This page shows what §15 actually looks like so the departure can be judged rather than assumed — if it is accepted, it belongs in the Figma file first. One side effect worth weighing: dark ink on a near-white surface clears AA on every run, where the gradient cards measure 472 of 530 text runs below it.

Two contradictions inside the brief, left visible rather than quietly resolved. §2 puts 6 Sections · 23 Enclosures in the header and §20 wants both as first-class widgets — so the same two numbers appear twice, which §27's own test would delete. I have built both as specified and made the header copies quiet (13px metadata) against the widgets' 34px, but the honest fix is to drop one. And §20 calls Sections and Enclosures first-class while §21's Facility Manager, Manager and Admin defaults omit them; here §21 wins and they sit in the Add panel for those three roles.

Content determines the height, and that is not free. The deck is a CSS grid with auto rows, so a band is as tall as its tallest widget — a row of metrics comes out ~150px and the Infrastructure row ~250px, with nothing stretched. The product's packer cannot do this: SPAN_TABLE gives fixed 164px rows, so a real card is 164 or 344 and never 150. Porting this means variable row heights in the packer, which touches the FLIP pass and the saved-layout contract — the largest single item in the brief.

What the numbers are. Everything is Mysuru Rescue Centre's own, from SITES['ms-rescue'] and its figures override: 6 / 23 / 41 / 28 / 147, 96 assets · 4 critical, 18 jobs · 7 overdue, 14 vet cases · 3 critical, 48 tasks · 19 due today, 19 of 25 compliance, power on generator, diesel reserve 31%. The brief's examples say 248 assets and 9 open jobs — those are Bannerghatta's, and drawing them here would reintroduce the per-Site bug the handoff warns about. Derived, not overridden: staff on site today (38), enclosures occupied (18, from 23 less 2 free and 3 restricted), Sections' per-section split, and Infrastructure's six system names — Mysuru's override names four states, and two more were needed to make §13's list worth its extra height.

Deliberately absent. No Site Health, score or condition gauge anywhere — health: 72 is on the record and is not drawn. No attention roll-up, no alert list, no chart, no trend, no "View →", and no controls inside any widget, which is why the three action shapes from widget-concepts.html do not appear on this page at all. They still hold for module pages, where §28 says the detail lives.
