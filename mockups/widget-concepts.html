Twelve compositions the catalogue does not have

Drawn from the four reference sheets in References/Widgets_Reference/. What is taken from them is the composition — the shape of the argument a card makes — and never the styling. Every card below is this product's own gradient, radius, shadow and type, at the artboard's own card sizes, filled with the reference Site's real figures.

The catalogue has twenty-one layouts today. Each concept here answers a question none of those twenty-one can, and the annotation beside it names which one you would otherwise reach for and why it falls short.

This is a mockup. Nothing is wired to derive(), the picker, the packer or the saved layout — no supportedSizes are enforced and no figure is computed. It exists to be judged and cut down before any of it is built. Two concepts are marked ⚠ because they are decisions rather than proposals: #11 would be the first card in the product that writes, and #12 overlaps timeline closely enough that you should pick one.

The contact sheet

All twelve on one grid, at real card size, at the four-column band. This is the brief's own test: before reading a single label, you should be able to tell that these are different kinds of card.

The concepts

One block each — the card at real size, then what it is for.

What this would cost to build

Twelve layouts is not twelve small jobs. Each one needs an entry in LAYOUTS, a block of CSS, a row in LAYOUT_FILL, real data on every widget that uses it, a place in at least one of the fifteen seeded role defaults, and a line in the overflow sweep at six widths. The last two are where the time goes: a composition nobody's default page carries is a composition the picker can offer and nobody will ever see, and the sweep is what stops a card being shipped with its title ellipsed at 900px.

The seed is the real constraint. Eight of these twelve are 2×2. The Site defaults were re-cut last session into blocks of ten cells precisely so two-row cards could land in them — but ten cells hold one 2×2 and one 2×1 with a hole, or two 2×2 and a spare column. Adding eight new 2×2 compositions to a page that already runs 46% stat means replacing cards in those defaults, not appending. Pick four.
