ANTZ Command Centre · module widgets

Widget ideas, module by module

Eighteen modules. Each gets a block of ten cells — the seed convention the Site defaults already follow — packed from mixed sizes, with a 2×2 signature card built for what that module uniquely holds. Every figure comes from docs/Modules.md Files/.

The three things the first pass got wrong are fixed on purpose. Sizes vary: 36 cards are 1×1, 28 are 2×1, 22 are 2×2, 3 are full width. A 1×1 carries no title — the module glyph names it, exactly as a door does. And no card repeats in words what its figure already said; what a card is for is in the legend under each block instead. Photographs and quick actions are in, at three sizes each.

How to read this, and what it would cost

Every block is exactly ten cells and packs with no hole. That constraint produced the size mix rather than a preference for it: ten cells hold 2×2 + 2×1 + 2×1 + 1×1 + 1×1 or 2×2 + 2×2 + 1×1 + 1×1 and little else, so a module with a signature card has to spend the rest of its block on small ones. That is exactly the pressure the real seeds are under.

41 compositions is the number to argue about. Eighteen are signatures that serve one module and would not transplant — Medical's severity ladder is not Housing's estate list — so they are eighteen separate pieces of CSS that earn their keep only if that module's page is worth designing. The other twenty-three are shared vocabulary: eight at 1×1, eleven at 2×1, one action shape at 2×2, three full width. If this has to shrink, cut signatures, not vocabulary — the vocabulary is what stops the catalogue being 46% one composition, and it is what the 1×1 and 2×1 slots in every seed will actually be filled with.

Photographs only where a photograph exists. Six real files in assets/img/, so six image cards and no more. A card that needs a picture of this tiger is a card that will be wrong. Everywhere else the module glyph is blown up behind the content at 12%, which is what entity already does.

What is still not answered. None of these is wired to derive(), so no figure moves when you switch Site or role, and no supportedSizes are enforced — several signatures would need a real answer for what they become at medium, and the honest answer for some (perimeter, month, matrix) is that they have no smaller form and should be offered at one size only.
