# Product

> Draft written by Claude on 18 Sep 2026 from the owner's direction in session; the owner has not yet confirmed it. Edit freely.

## Register

product

## Users
Zoo and rescue-centre staff on an iPad or a desk browser, several times a day, between rounds: the site manager and curator (the whole site at a glance, what needs a decision), vets and para-vets (who is sick, what is due, what the lab owes), keepers (their section's animals, tasks, diets), and the director (compliance, the collection, trends across sites). Some see one site, some several; the same page must speak for one site or for all of them.

## Product Purpose
The ANTZ Command Centre home: the first screen after sign-in, where the day's state of the collection is read in a minute and every module is one tap away. It exists so that nobody has to open five modules to learn whether anything is wrong this morning. Success is a keeper or a curator opening it, seeing the two or three things that need them, and going straight there.

## Brand Personality
Calm, exact, alive. It is a working tool for people who care about animals: warm without being decorative, dense without being noisy, honest about what the data is and is not.

## Anti-references
- Tables of numbers with nothing to look at ("boring", the owner's word) — every data set earns its own presentation.
- Loud category colouring (a blue for birds, a green for mammals, a coral for reptiles) — one palette, meaning carried by words and position.
- Emoji, decorative florals, and anything that reads as a consumer app.
- The everything-on-one-screen dashboard: the home stays light; detail lives one tap in.

## Design Principles
- One glance, then one tap: the home shows counts and what needs action; the report page shows the rest, rendered only when opened.
- Every figure is arithmetic on rows: no total that another view could disagree with.
- Say it in words as well as colour: a late count is coral AND says "late".
- Scope is explicit: the page always says which site it is speaking for.
- Ages well: prose comments explain why, tokens not hardcodes, nothing pretends to be wired when it is not.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA for text contrast; every chart has a text equivalent (an aria-label or the figures beside it); chips and toggles carry `aria-pressed`; pages are keyboard-operable with Escape to close; reduced motion honoured on every transition. Touch targets 44px on the iPad.
