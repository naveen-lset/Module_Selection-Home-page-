## User-visible acceptance, per thing shipped

- **The module launcher (LOCAL ONLY).** Serve the repo and open
  `/index.html?v=2`, press **Quick Actions**. You should see nineteen coloured
  module tiles fly out of the pill onto a white frosted panel, 4 across. If you
  see sixteen grey verb cells instead, `QA_CONTENT` in index.html is set to
  `'verbs'`. If the panel is empty, `DOOR_IDS` is reading `d[0]` again instead
  of `d.moduleId` — `DOORS` is a list of variant objects, not tuples, and that
  failure is silent.
- **The launcher at narrow widths.** At 620px it should drop to 3 columns, at
  480px to 2 and scroll inside the panel. If its top edge is off-screen, the
  `maxHeight` clamp in `show()` has gone back to being computed from the pill's
  top rather than the panel's own bottom.
- **The white backdrop (LOCAL ONLY).** Opening the launcher should brighten and
  blur the page behind, not darken it. If it darkens, you are looking at the
  deployed `bcaf295` and not your working copy.
- **Liquid glass pill and disc (LIVE).** Load the alias. The pill is white at
  40% with a black label; the chat disc is 56px and cyan. **Scroll V1 until a
  coral observation card sits behind the foot** — the disc must stay cyan. If it
  turns orange, the disc's tint has been "restored" to the node's 20/40; it is
  55/35 on purpose and `verify_v2` says why.
- **The pill's glyph (LIVE).** Its ink box should measure 18×18 inside a 20px
  container. If it looks 9% large, the mask is back to `100% 100%`.
- **`cdp.py`'s profile fix (UNCOMMITTED).** Run any verifier, then
  `ls -d $TMPDIR/cdp-profile-*`. Expect none. If they pile up, the `rmtree` in
  `Chrome.quit()` is gone — this filled the disk once and blocked every command.
- **Verifier honesty.** `python3 tools/verify_v2.py <base>` four times running
  should be four `ALL PASS`. If it alternates, the freeze is sleeping toward the
  animations again instead of awaiting them.
